Quick answers

Do I need my own avatar?

No. SonaPin includes an interactive blue wolf. You can import a compatible VRM whenever you’re ready.

What does the QR code share?

You choose a website, social profile, contact card, or custom text. Preview it and scan it with another phone before you share your badge.

Where is my profile and avatar stored?

Your badge details and imported avatar are kept on your device. Read the privacy policy for details about optional crash reports in Sentry-enabled builds.
