A badge with a pulse.

Version 1 is being built around one focused job: making your character easy to share while keeping the moment personal.

01 / AVATAR

Bring your character into the room.

Start with the built-in demo or import a compatible VRM avatar. SonaPin will report what the model supports before it replaces your working badge.

02 / IDENTITY

Show up as yourself.

Add your display name, pronouns, species or character type, and an optional short tagline.

03 / CONNECTION

Share one clear next step.

Create a high-contrast QR code for a website, social profile, contact link, or custom text, then enlarge it for easier scanning.

04 / CONTROL

Convention-floor friendly.

Planned controls include an accidental-edit lock, optional keep-awake behavior, adjustable interactions, haptics, reduced motion, and high-contrast QR presentation.

Pin it in three moves.

A resumable setup flow keeps the choices clear and saves progress as you go.

  1. Choose a face

    Try the built-in demo or review a compatible VRM model from your Files app.

  2. Build your badge

    Add identity details, pick a theme, and validate the link you want people to scan.

  3. Enter badge mode

    Keep the avatar expressive, the QR quiet zone clear, and editing safely locked.

Local means local

Your character stays with you.

The version 1 design has no SonaPin account or backend. Imported models, badge fields, QR settings, and preferences stay in the app’s local container.
