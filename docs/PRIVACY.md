# SonaPin privacy policy draft

**Effective date:** Not yet published  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-12

SonaPin is a local-first iOS app from MrDemonWolf, Inc. Version 1 does not collect personal data from the app.

## Data you add

SonaPin lets you add badge profile fields, a QR payload and QR preferences, and an optional VRM avatar. This information is stored on your device. The app does not upload it to MrDemonWolf, Inc. or a SonaPin server.

Your QR code is meant to be visible to people near you. Anyone who can see or photograph it may read its contents. Put only information in the QR code that you intend to share.

## Accounts, analytics, and advertising

Version 1 has:

- no account or login;
- no analytics or advertising SDK;
- no tracking;
- no model upload;
- no public gallery, feed, or comments; and
- no background networking.

## Apple services and Files

When you choose a model, Apple's document picker gives SonaPin temporary access to the file you selected. SonaPin validates it and may copy an app-managed version into its private Application Support container. Your use of iCloud Drive or another Files provider is governed by that provider's terms and privacy practices.

Apple may process App Store purchase, crash, and diagnostic information under Apple's own policies and your device-sharing settings. SonaPin does not receive an in-app user account from that processing.

## Storage and deletion

Your profile, QR settings, preferences, compatibility report, and imported avatar remain locally until you change them, remove them, use Delete All Local Data, or uninstall the app. Device backups may retain app data according to your Apple backup settings.

## Children

SonaPin does not knowingly collect children's personal information because version 1 sends no user-entered app data to the developer. A parent or guardian should help a child avoid placing private contact information in a visible QR code.

## Changes

If a later version adds networking, sharing, analytics, accounts, or another data practice, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the support contact published at `https://mrdemonwolf.github.io/sonapin-ios/support/`.

This draft must be updated with a direct privacy email or mailing contact and published at a stable HTTPS URL before App Store submission.
